How to manually open App Store?

  1. Make sure your device is running iOS 14.0 or later
  2. If in WeChat, first click the top right corner to open in browser
  3. Click the "Manual Redirect" button above
  4. When prompted by the system, select "Open" to launch App Store
  5. Tap the "Get" or "Download" button in App Store to install
